The Reality Shift
A guided process to help you
transform your painful thoughts.
The beliefs and statements we hold about reality shape how we experience life. They tell us what's true, what's possible, what we deserve — and they determine the quality of our relationship with reality itself.
That relationship is everything. When we're in conflict with reality, we suffer. When we're at peace with it, we're free — not because anything outside has changed, but because our perspective has.
This process works by asking simple questions. Nothing should feel forced. Approach it like a meditation — answer as honestly and truthfully as you can at every point. The questions do the work.
Find somewhere quiet. This takes about 15–20 minutes.
